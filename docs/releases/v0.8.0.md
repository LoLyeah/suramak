0.8.0 is the release where the session limit stopped being my machine's number and became yours. The 45- and 60-minute caps in 0.7.0 came from measurements on a 3.7 GiB 2-core box plus a memory budget I invented; the app now asks the OS how much memory it has and derives the limit from that, and refuses a model that cannot fit instead of quietly switching to a smaller one.

The second change is where the audio lives. Decoding streams to a temp file in bounded chunks and reads it back through `mmap` — a 30-minute 48 kHz stereo import peaked at 578 MiB instead of 1,105 MiB, with byte-identical transcripts.

Also in this release: a job the OS kills twice is quarantined rather than resumed forever, and the 15-minute macro-window path was built, measured, and **shipped disabled** because it moved segmentation by 7 % on natural speech. The numbers are in `docs/reports/M26c-macro-windows.md`.
