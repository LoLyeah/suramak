Suramak — on-device transcription.

Record or import audio (WhatsApp voice notes, Ogg/WebM, mic) and transcribe it entirely on this phone: no account, no cloud, no telemetry.

• English + Indonesian transcription (Whisper on-device; the model downloads on first use)
• Correct in the editor: split/merge segments with undo, reversible cleanup rules, a personal glossary
• Search the whole library instantly
• Export SRT/VTT/ASS/Markdown/JSON/TXT; optional bilingual subtitles through your own endpoint
• Word-level playback highlighting
• Foreground-service transcription with the screen off; kill-safe resume

GPL-3.0, source at github.com/LoLyeah/suramak.
